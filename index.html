Our Story

A Century of Refinement

Since 1924, Aurélia Grand Hotel has been the chosen home of discerning travellers, dignitaries, and artists who seek not merely accommodation, but an experience that transcends the ordinary.

Each suite is a curated world of its own — where antique craftsmanship meets contemporary comfort, and every detail has been considered with devotion to the art of hospitality.
